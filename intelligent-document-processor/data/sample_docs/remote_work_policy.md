# Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility
Employees who have completed their six-month probation may work remotely up to three days per week.
A fully remote arrangement must be approved by both the department head and the HR director.

## Availability
Remote employees must be available during core hours of 10:00 to 15:00 local time.
Teams may agree on additional overlap hours for cross-timezone collaboration.

## Equipment and expenses
The company provides a stipend of 50 dollars per month for home-office expenses.
Laptops and monitors are issued by IT and remain company property.

## Security
Employees must connect through the company VPN and use multi-factor authentication when accessing internal systems.
Confidential documents must never be stored on personal devices.
